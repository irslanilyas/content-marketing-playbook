# Content Marketing Playbook

Write content that gets attention, builds trust and ends in one clear action. Load only the reference file the task needs.

## Rules
- Posting is not marketing. Every piece moves someone through: Attention > Interest > Trust > Action.
- Know the audience first. Content for everyone lands with no one.
- Use real specifics: numbers, names of things, moments, the audience's own words. Never invent facts, results or quotes. If a detail is missing, ask or leave a clear placeholder.
- Plain words. Short lines. No em dashes. Avoid filler like "game-changer", "unlock", "delve", "leverage", "in today's fast-paced world".
- One idea per piece. One call to action.

## Workflow
1. Get the basics: who it is for, the problem, why they should care, what they should do next. Ask at most two short questions if missing, otherwise assume and state the assumption.
2. Pick the format and platform.
3. Write 3 to 5 hooks first. Test each: does it stop the scroll, name the audience or problem, and create a gap the viewer wants closed?
4. Write the body using a story or format template.
5. Add a re-hook where attention drops, then one CTA.
6. Run the checklist below. Give the person the final copy, not a lecture.

## Where to look
- Hooks (107 structures by category): `references/hooks-library.md`
- Re-hooks to hold attention mid-piece: `references/re-hooks.md`
- Story scripts (10 storytelling formats with fill-in flows): `references/story-templates.md`
- 100 content formats (advice, easy, opinion, relatable, storytelling): `references/formats-library.md`
- Funnel, creator notes and reel hook groups: `references/playbook-notes.md`

## Hook levels
Level 1 names the topic. Level 2 adds a specific result or tension. Level 3 adds a surprising angle, a contrast or a stake. Aim for Level 3.

## Angle system
Take one topic and change the angle: mistake, myth, before/after, behind the scenes, step-by-step, opinion, story, comparison. Same topic, many posts.

## Quality checklist
- Hook is specific and in the first line.
- Written for one clear person.
- Every claim is true or marked as a placeholder.
- No banned filler words, no em dashes.
- Ends with one CTA that matches the stage of the funnel.
- Reads naturally out loud.

## Private data
If files like `your-best-posts.md`, `your-stories.md`, `audience-language.md`, `your-opinions.md`, `good-vs-bad.md` or `never-write.md` exist next to this skill, read them first and copy their voice over any generic pattern.

# Creator playbook notes

- **Funnel:** Posting is not marketing. Attention \> Interest \> Trust \> Action.
- **Ask:** who is it for, what problem, why care, what next. Biggest mistake: content for everyone.
- **Fail points:** weak hook, no value, poor retention, weak CTA.
- **Hook levels:** bad "5 tips to lose weight", good "Here's how to lose weight faster", great "If I had to lose weight again, this is exactly what I'd do" (curiosity + personal experience). Business great hook: "The reason you're not getting clients has almost nothing to do with how good your service is."
- **Hook types (16):** controversial, mistake, step-by-step, myth-busting, challenge, relatable pain, fear-based, what-if, trend-based, emotion-driven, secret, before/after (+ story, advice/warning, curiosity groups).
- **Angle system (50 angles from 1 topic):** problem / solve it / go deeper / show proof / change perspective.
- **Storytelling:** telling = events, details, sequence. Storytelling = emotion, meaning, connection.
- **Algorithm (the creator's own claims, not verified):** content = reach, watch time + likes + sends = distribution, sends matter most for non-followers, originality over reposting. Trial Reels test with strangers first. Keywords in profile/captions. Collabs. Hashtags do not fix weak content.
- **Method:** save 50 top creators' videos over 2M views, find patterns, rebuild with own experience.
- **Carousel rules:** big cover claim with number, one idea per slide, Hook \> P1 \> P2 \> P3 \> CTA, Z-pattern, minimal, brand colors, 1080x1440, "Comment WORD" CTA.

## Reel idea hook groups

**Groups:** Instant hooks, Tips/tools/hacks, Beginners/how-to, Storytelling/mindset. Pattern: topic blank + number/time limit/mistake/"I" moment.

# 30 ways to re-hook a viewer

**Idea:** retention drops over time, so insert a new "hook" every few seconds to lift the retention curve. CTA: comment "Gift" for a free Proven Viral Hooks Database (hook template, views, niche, hook type).

**Re-hook list (verbal)**

- 1 Ask a question ("Why does this happen?")
- 2 Add a problem ("But there's one problem...")
- 3 Tease what's coming ("Wait until you see the final result.")
- 4 Show proof ("Here's the proof.")
- 5 Add a twist ("But then something weird happened.")
- 6 Show a comment (bring a comment on screen)
- 7 Make a comparison ("A vs. B.")
- 8 Reveal a mistake ("Here's where I went wrong.")
- 9 Show before and after
- 10 Warn them ("Don't do this yet.")
- 11 Reveal a number ("Only 3% did this.")
- 12 Challenge them ("Can you spot the mistake?")
- 13 Hold back the answer ("Before I tell you why...")
- 14 Show an example ("Here's what I mean.")
- 15 Correct yourself ("Actually... that's wrong.")
- 16 Raise the stakes ("And it gets worse.")
- 17 Reveal something hidden
- 18 Add an exception ("Unless you're doing this...")
- 19 Make a prediction ("Here's what happens next.")
- 20 Add another benefit ("But that's not even the best part.")

**Visual re-hooks**

- 21 switch the shot (change camera angle)
- 22 cut to B-roll
- 23 use a prop
- 24 zoom in on an important moment
- 25 change location
- 26 switch format (talking head to screen recording)
- 27 bring in another person
- 28 pause before the payoff
- 29 call back to the hook ("Remember what I said earlier?")
- 30 open another question ("But that leaves one question...")

**Text-post translation:** the same ideas work as line breaks in LinkedIn posts (open loop, twist, stakes, "but that's not the best part").

# Short-form story templates (10 formats)

All 10 formats are 1st-person POV. Every script starts with a Hook (from the Visual/Spoken/Text hook database).

#### 1. HERO'S JOURNEY

(problem \> solution) Secret sauce: viewer must emotionally relate to the common pain; if they feel it, they care about the solution.

**Flow**

- Hook
- character + problem
- failed attempts (exaggerate problem)
- novel solution
- why it worked + success
- optional CTA.

**Mad-lib**

- INTRO "X years ago I \[problem\]." / "I was a \[role\] struggling with \[problem\], no idea how to fix it."
- INFLECTION "Because of this I was \[pain\]." / "It got so bad that I \[lowest moment\]."
- RISING "I tried \[#1\], \[#2\], \[#3\], nothing worked." / "For X months I did everything they said: \[advice\]. Still no results."
- CLIMAX "Finally I figured out the one thing that worked: \[solution\]." / "Then I discovered \[x\], and everything changed."
- FALLING "When I started doing this I \[result\]." / "Within \[timeframe\] I went from \[before\] to \[after\]."
- RESOLUTION "Now I help \[audience\] go from \[pain\] to \[dream result\]. \[CTA\]."

#### 2. PERSONAL LEARNING / EPIPHANY

Lead with proof, then teach. Secret sauce: very strong visible proof (screenshots) = "how did they do that" curiosity, AND a non-obvious solution, else proof looks like an unrepeatable outlier.

**Flow**

- Hook
- desired result with proof
- process breakdown
- optional "why this differs from common advice"
- CTA.

**Templates**

- "I \[result\] in \[timeframe\], here's exactly how." / "This one change got me \[result\]." / "\[Screenshot\]. Everyone keeps asking how I did this."
- BACKSTORY "Before this I was \[state\] and thought \[old belief\]."
- INSIGHT "Then I realized \[x\]." / "The mistake was \[x\]. The fix was \[y\]."
- BREAKDOWN "First \[1\], then \[2\], finally \[3\]."
- CTA "comment LEARN / link in bio".

#### 3. ABOUT ME

(origin story) Pin it to the profile; builds personal trust more than expertise.

**Flow**

- Hook
- normal life
- epiphany/change moment
- how it changed your path
- optional CTA.

**Templates**

- INTRO "Hi I'm \[name\], X years ago I was a normal \[role\] living a normal life."
- CONFLICT "Well, all up until \[event\]."
- EPIPHANY "Because of this I realized \[x\]."
- CHANGE "So I \[bold action\]. Fast forward to today, I've \[result\] and \[lifestyle change\]."
- PURPOSE "\[Dream result\] changed my life, so I made it my mission to help \[audience\] go from \[pain\] to \[result\]."

#### 4. BEFORE & AFTER

Power = size of the gap. Bigger, more surprising gap = more watch time, saves, shares. Sync the transition to a beat drop. Keep 15-30 seconds; needs little explanation.

**Flow**

- Hook
- before state
- brief tension/countdown
- after reveal synced to audio
- optional time/effort context
- optional CTA.

**Templates**

- "\[X months\] of \[effort\] in \[X seconds\]." / "This is what \[subject\] looked like before vs after \[action\]." Before: "\[X ago\] this was my reality." After: "\[X later\]." Context: "All it took was \[action\] for \[timeframe\]." CTA: "Follow for the full breakdown."

#### 5. GOAL / DREAM JOURNEY

(open-ended, unlike Challenge) Dream must feel ambitious enough that people wonder if you'll make it; share vulnerable moments; feels like letting viewer in on something personal.

**Flow**

- Hook
- dream + origin
- moment you decided to pursue it
- progress update
- what's next (milestones, deadline)
- CTA to follow along.

**Templates**

- "For as long as I can remember I've wanted to \[dream\]."
- "Then X weeks ago I stopped talking about it and \[first action\]. I was terrified, but did it anyway."
- "Here's where I'm at: \[progress\]. Next \[step\]. Deadline \[date\]."
- CTA "comment YES if you think I can do it" / "document the whole thing, hold me accountable."

#### 6. CHALLENGE

(bounded, with rules) Make the objective extremely grand; add high-intensity complications. Flow: Hook \> context/rules \> plot arc with obstacles \> resolution or cliffhanger.

**Templates**

- "I gave myself \[X days\] to \[challenge\]. No shortcuts."
- "The rules: \[1\],\[2\],\[3\]."
- "Day X: \[setback\]. Then \[complication\] happened and I almost \[near-failure\]."
- "Final result: \[outcome\]." or "With X hours left... follow for part 2."

#### 7. WIN

(victory announcement) Proof must be undeniable and visual (screenshot, notification). Keep short, raw, spontaneous. Not a how-to, not a general update.

**Flow**

- Hook states the win
- proof
- emotional beat
- optional journey nod
- optional CTA.

**Templates**

- "It finally happened: \[win\]." / "We just \[achievement\] and I'm still in shock."
- "Here's the screenshot. X months ago this number was \[old\]."
- "I almost quit \[when\]. This is why I didn't."
- "Next goal: \[milestone\]. Follow to watch."

#### 8. DAY IN THE LIFE

Sell your personality or the life you have; show something different and unexpected, not the standard routine.

**Flow**

- Hook (identity/context)
- context
- complication/tension
- resolution or thoughts
- optional CTA.

**Templates**

- "A day in the life of a \[age\] year old \[role\]." / "This is what it actually looks like to \[aspiration\]."
- "First thing: \[action\]. Most people don't know I \[unexpected detail\]."
- "Midway through \[task\], \[problem\] happened so I \[pivot\]."
- "End of day. Here's what I got done. Tomorrow \[next\]. It's not perfect, but it's \[honest feeling\]."

#### 9. PERSONAL UPDATE

Behind the curtain; for community, not authority. Flow: Hook teaser \> context \> the update with tension \> rationale.

**Templates**

- "I've been keeping this quiet for X weeks, but it's time."
- "You might remember I was \[previous\]. Well..."
- "I've decided to \[decision\]. It wasn't easy because \[tension\]."
- "At the end of the day \[value\] matters more to me than \[tradeoff\]."

#### 10. LESSON FROM OTHERS

(mentor story) Creator is narrator/student/curator, not hero. Make the other person a real character: name them, specific situation, lesson as a direct quote or vivid moment.

**Flow**

- Hook
- who they are + why they matter
- their struggle (vivid)
- the lesson
- how you applied it + what changed
- CTA.

**Templates**

- "Here's the best advice \[figure\] ever gave me."
- "They were about to close the business / starting over from nothing."
- "They told me: '\[quote\].'"
- "I took that advice and \[action\]. Within \[timeframe\] I \[outcome\]."
- CTA "save it, you'll need it on a hard day" / "Who taught you the most? Comment below."

**Cross-format rules:** lead with proof or the pain; relatability before expertise; name specifics (numbers, timeframes, names); CTA is optional and comes last; vulnerability moments ("I almost quit", "I was terrified") carry the story.

### STORYTELLING (10 formats)

**CORE STORY STRUCTURE (used in all)**

- **NORMAL:** the old life or struggle (relatable pain).
- **EXPLOSION:** the turning point, decision or realization.
- **NEW NORMAL:** payoff, growth, honest takeaway.

Keep it to one specific problem, document the process, and end with a CTA that opens a new loop ("follow to see what I got").

**Long-form personal brand script structure ("This is \[name\]")**

- HOOK ("This is \[name\]")
- CONTEXT with tension and one authentic emotion (spite etc.) creating a curiosity gap (e.g. "19-year-old unemployed dropout")
- EXPLOSION (decision that moved you from struggle to now)
- YOUR NICHE/JOURNEY (what you're doing now, specifics tied to your page)
- GOAL/MISSION (why)
- CONNECTION (state a shared goal with the audience, e.g. an alliance) -- most important for converting viewers to followers
- CALL TO ACTION (what they get by following). Roughly 3 hours to make; B-roll quality is not important.

#### S3 "Guys I haven't been to my math class in 4 weeks and the midterm is in 3 hours"

- high stakes + curiosity gap
- walk through the steps (hour-by-hour plan), normal/explosion/new normal, "follow to see what I got" opens a loop. Script focus \> fancy editing. B-roll just supports what you say.

#### S4

- POV hook ("POV: you're single, have no friends, live alone...") + running timestamp overlay (6:54PM...) while a voice-over tells a vulnerable life chapter (not describing what is on screen)
- label "part 1" to set up a series. Concrete specifics of hardship build trust.

#### S6 "I had everything a nice \[identity\] was supposed to be happy with"

- names an identity to attract that group
- two curiosity gaps (what does he have, why is he unhappy)
- every B-roll proves each line
- suggested to keep to 3 failures then the thing that works.

#### S7

**Storytelling principles:** introduce yourself fast, name tension, show proof visually, keep one lesson, tell it from your own perspective, use age/time stamps to signal a payoff is coming, end with an open loop or shared goal.
