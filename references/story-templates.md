# Short-form story templates (10 formats)

All 10 formats are 1st-person POV. Every script starts with a Hook (from the Visual/Spoken/Text hook database).

#### 1. HERO'S JOURNEY

(problem \> solution) Secret sauce: viewer must emotionally relate to the common pain; if they feel it, they care about the solution.

**Flow**

- Hook
- character + problem
- failed attempts (exaggerate problem)
- novel solution
- why it worked + success
- optional CTA.

**Mad-lib**

- INTRO "X years ago I \[problem\]." / "I was a \[role\] struggling with \[problem\], no idea how to fix it."
- INFLECTION "Because of this I was \[pain\]." / "It got so bad that I \[lowest moment\]."
- RISING "I tried \[#1\], \[#2\], \[#3\], nothing worked." / "For X months I did everything they said: \[advice\]. Still no results."
- CLIMAX "Finally I figured out the one thing that worked: \[solution\]." / "Then I discovered \[x\], and everything changed."
- FALLING "When I started doing this I \[result\]." / "Within \[timeframe\] I went from \[before\] to \[after\]."
- RESOLUTION "Now I help \[audience\] go from \[pain\] to \[dream result\]. \[CTA\]."

#### 2. PERSONAL LEARNING / EPIPHANY

Lead with proof, then teach. Secret sauce: very strong visible proof (screenshots) = "how did they do that" curiosity, AND a non-obvious solution, else proof looks like an unrepeatable outlier.

**Flow**

- Hook
- desired result with proof
- process breakdown
- optional "why this differs from common advice"
- CTA.

**Templates**

- "I \[result\] in \[timeframe\], here's exactly how." / "This one change got me \[result\]." / "\[Screenshot\]. Everyone keeps asking how I did this."
- BACKSTORY "Before this I was \[state\] and thought \[old belief\]."
- INSIGHT "Then I realized \[x\]." / "The mistake was \[x\]. The fix was \[y\]."
- BREAKDOWN "First \[1\], then \[2\], finally \[3\]."
- CTA "comment LEARN / link in bio".

#### 3. ABOUT ME

(origin story) Pin it to the profile; builds personal trust more than expertise.

**Flow**

- Hook
- normal life
- epiphany/change moment
- how it changed your path
- optional CTA.

**Templates**

- INTRO "Hi I'm \[name\], X years ago I was a normal \[role\] living a normal life."
- CONFLICT "Well, all up until \[event\]."
- EPIPHANY "Because of this I realized \[x\]."
- CHANGE "So I \[bold action\]. Fast forward to today, I've \[result\] and \[lifestyle change\]."
- PURPOSE "\[Dream result\] changed my life, so I made it my mission to help \[audience\] go from \[pain\] to \[result\]."

#### 4. BEFORE & AFTER

Power = size of the gap. Bigger, more surprising gap = more watch time, saves, shares. Sync the transition to a beat drop. Keep 15-30 seconds; needs little explanation.

**Flow**

- Hook
- before state
- brief tension/countdown
- after reveal synced to audio
- optional time/effort context
- optional CTA.

**Templates**

- "\[X months\] of \[effort\] in \[X seconds\]." / "This is what \[subject\] looked like before vs after \[action\]." Before: "\[X ago\] this was my reality." After: "\[X later\]." Context: "All it took was \[action\] for \[timeframe\]." CTA: "Follow for the full breakdown."

#### 5. GOAL / DREAM JOURNEY

(open-ended, unlike Challenge) Dream must feel ambitious enough that people wonder if you'll make it; share vulnerable moments; feels like letting viewer in on something personal.

**Flow**

- Hook
- dream + origin
- moment you decided to pursue it
- progress update
- what's next (milestones, deadline)
- CTA to follow along.

**Templates**

- "For as long as I can remember I've wanted to \[dream\]."
- "Then X weeks ago I stopped talking about it and \[first action\]. I was terrified, but did it anyway."
- "Here's where I'm at: \[progress\]. Next \[step\]. Deadline \[date\]."
- CTA "comment YES if you think I can do it" / "document the whole thing, hold me accountable."

#### 6. CHALLENGE

(bounded, with rules) Make the objective extremely grand; add high-intensity complications. Flow: Hook \> context/rules \> plot arc with obstacles \> resolution or cliffhanger.

**Templates**

- "I gave myself \[X days\] to \[challenge\]. No shortcuts."
- "The rules: \[1\],\[2\],\[3\]."
- "Day X: \[setback\]. Then \[complication\] happened and I almost \[near-failure\]."
- "Final result: \[outcome\]." or "With X hours left... follow for part 2."

#### 7. WIN

(victory announcement) Proof must be undeniable and visual (screenshot, notification). Keep short, raw, spontaneous. Not a how-to, not a general update.

**Flow**

- Hook states the win
- proof
- emotional beat
- optional journey nod
- optional CTA.

**Templates**

- "It finally happened: \[win\]." / "We just \[achievement\] and I'm still in shock."
- "Here's the screenshot. X months ago this number was \[old\]."
- "I almost quit \[when\]. This is why I didn't."
- "Next goal: \[milestone\]. Follow to watch."

#### 8. DAY IN THE LIFE

Sell your personality or the life you have; show something different and unexpected, not the standard routine.

**Flow**

- Hook (identity/context)
- context
- complication/tension
- resolution or thoughts
- optional CTA.

**Templates**

- "A day in the life of a \[age\] year old \[role\]." / "This is what it actually looks like to \[aspiration\]."
- "First thing: \[action\]. Most people don't know I \[unexpected detail\]."
- "Midway through \[task\], \[problem\] happened so I \[pivot\]."
- "End of day. Here's what I got done. Tomorrow \[next\]. It's not perfect, but it's \[honest feeling\]."

#### 9. PERSONAL UPDATE

Behind the curtain; for community, not authority. Flow: Hook teaser \> context \> the update with tension \> rationale.

**Templates**

- "I've been keeping this quiet for X weeks, but it's time."
- "You might remember I was \[previous\]. Well..."
- "I've decided to \[decision\]. It wasn't easy because \[tension\]."
- "At the end of the day \[value\] matters more to me than \[tradeoff\]."

#### 10. LESSON FROM OTHERS

(mentor story) Creator is narrator/student/curator, not hero. Make the other person a real character: name them, specific situation, lesson as a direct quote or vivid moment.

**Flow**

- Hook
- who they are + why they matter
- their struggle (vivid)
- the lesson
- how you applied it + what changed
- CTA.

**Templates**

- "Here's the best advice \[figure\] ever gave me."
- "They were about to close the business / starting over from nothing."
- "They told me: '\[quote\].'"
- "I took that advice and \[action\]. Within \[timeframe\] I \[outcome\]."
- CTA "save it, you'll need it on a hard day" / "Who taught you the most? Comment below."

**Cross-format rules:** lead with proof or the pain; relatability before expertise; name specifics (numbers, timeframes, names); CTA is optional and comes last; vulnerability moments ("I almost quit", "I was terrified") carry the story.
