# 100 viral formats library

**Universal rules:** "Follow the b-roll as much as possible"; hook = specific audience/number/pain; caption short, numbered, save prompt, engagement question at end; credit original creator if inspired.

### ADVICE (25 formats): hook template then caption pattern

#### A1 "Strange advice from the internet I laughed at until it worked"

- 5 unusual tips, each with 1 short personal result
- unusual \> obvious
- end "what do you think?"

#### A2

- No-hook visual
- "camera is dirty let me clean it" bit, then positive message synced to music
- short 1-2 line caption.

#### A3 "I'm 32 and I wish I invested in these 5 assets in my 20s"

- age + regret + clear topic
- each point: what, examples, why, how to start
- beginner terms only.

#### A4 "I want to travel but it's too expensive. Welcome to my page, I got you"

- objection + warm confident line
- numbered list (15 hacks), 1-2 lines each, end with engagement question.

#### A5 "If you're 25-35, start these 11 habits"

- broad (not niche) advice from real experience
- open with own story, close with reflective line ("life isn't happening to you, it's happening for you").

#### A6

- Split screen "broke musician vs \$10k musician"
- X and checkmark per side
- myths vs truths
- strongest point first
- 1-2 sentences each.

#### A7

- Calm talking-to-camera "Daily reminder"

- slow tone
- something that surprised you, contradicts a belief
- "you" language
- filmed in a car for vulnerability.

#### A8 "AI vibe coding changed my life"

- one intriguing sentence, personal moment makes people ask how/why
- bullet points not script
- clarity over sounding smart
- mood matches setting.

#### A9 "21 things to do instead of rotting on your phone"

- numbered list title, low-energy, "you" phrasing per point, hashtags.

#### A10 "50 foods you can overeat without getting fat"

#### A11 "3 things that will keep you broke in your 20s"

### STORYTELLING (10 formats)

**CORE STORY STRUCTURE (used in all)**

- **NORMAL:** the old life or struggle (relatable pain).
- **EXPLOSION:** the turning point, decision or realization.
- **NEW NORMAL:** payoff, growth, honest takeaway.

Keep it to one specific problem, document the process, and end with a CTA that opens a new loop ("follow to see what I got").

**Long-form personal brand script structure ("This is \[name\]")**

- HOOK ("This is \[name\]")
- CONTEXT with tension and one authentic emotion (spite etc.) creating a curiosity gap (e.g. "19-year-old unemployed dropout")
- EXPLOSION (decision that moved you from struggle to now)
- YOUR NICHE/JOURNEY (what you're doing now, specifics tied to your page)
- GOAL/MISSION (why)
- CONNECTION (state a shared goal with the audience, e.g. an alliance) -- most important for converting viewers to followers
- CALL TO ACTION (what they get by following). Roughly 3 hours to make; B-roll quality is not important.

#### S3 "Guys I haven't been to my math class in 4 weeks and the midterm is in 3 hours"

- high stakes + curiosity gap
- walk through the steps (hour-by-hour plan), normal/explosion/new normal, "follow to see what I got" opens a loop. Script focus \> fancy editing. B-roll just supports what you say.

#### S4

- POV hook ("POV: you're single, have no friends, live alone...") + running timestamp overlay (6:54PM...) while a voice-over tells a vulnerable life chapter (not describing what is on screen)
- label "part 1" to set up a series. Concrete specifics of hardship build trust.

#### S6 "I had everything a nice \[identity\] was supposed to be happy with"

- names an identity to attract that group
- two curiosity gaps (what does he have, why is he unhappy)
- every B-roll proves each line
- suggested to keep to 3 failures then the thing that works.

#### S7

- Age-stamped text ("17: Mom, there's a bad feeling in my stomach...", 18, 20, 22, 25, 28) over photos from different life stages
- sad old photos for Normal, lighter for Explosion, smiling for New Normal
- close "Welcome to my journey".

#### S8

- "She's way too positive. Bet she never struggles." + age-by-age list (15 years old: work at a gas station ... 2025 27 years old: 4th year nursing) with photos and camera-roll dates as undeniable proof
- choose the story closest to your niche pillar so profile visitors follow
- raw editing is fine.

#### S9

- Age-stamped dialogue with a parent ("13: dad, why do we have to move...") then gratitude then achievements ("dad, I became a specialist")
- full story in caption
- old blurry photos for Normal, recent achievement visuals for New Normal.

#### S10

- Series hook "\[Topic\] tips pt 1"
- one big lesson from one specific event, told from your own perspective
- remove noise (too many ideas confuse)
- close "send this to your partner / comment if you agree or disagree".

**Storytelling principles:** introduce yourself fast, name tension, show proof visually, keep one lesson, tell it from your own perspective, use age/time stamps to signal a payoff is coming, end with an open loop or shared goal.

### Part 4 (continued): more formats

- **Easy \#1 "Things that are NORMAL as \[niche noun\]":** caption names the gap between social media and reality, gives an honest example, encouraging close, soft follow CTA. Research by searching "things that are normal as a \[niche noun\]".

- **Opinion \#17 "You vs. You":** ghost overlay of a more productive you at reduced opacity; caption "2026 you vs 2025 you".
- **Opinion \#20 "just use chatgpt it's easier":** argue AGAINST the opening statement; actionable caption steps; show the hard things you did.
- Opinion \#21 "Good things take time / I wish I had better skin": opinion then wish, before/after proof, a payoff coming.
- **Opinion \#22 "The silent flexes in life":** keep first 5-10s same, add your own flexes; read text aloud to check pacing; matching faces/emotion plus breathing shots (sunset, books).
- Opinion \#23 "What people think is embarrassing:": fear of judgement; caption "You will never be judged by somebody doing more than you"; show your niche action; "let them hate".
- **Opinion \#24 "Be soft, but not weak":** pairs of "X, but not Y" (listen but don't judge, enjoy but don't attach, grateful but don't settle); b-roll proves each.

Use the original video as timeline base for b-roll timing; caption introduces who you are.
